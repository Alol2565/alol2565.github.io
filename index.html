About

I build machine-learning systems for next-generation wireless networks — turning slow, hand-tuned physical models into fast, data-driven ones.

I'm a PhD candidate at Northeastern's WiNES Lab, where I work on AI/ML for 5G/6G systems: wireless digital twins, O-RAN, channel modeling, and real-time GPU/FPGA testbeds. My deep-learning channel models run 7000× faster than ray tracing (30 s → 4 ms, <4% error) and are deployed on Colosseum, the world's largest channel emulator. In summer 2026 I'm a Software Engineer intern at Apple on the Connectivity, Cellular & Satellite team.
